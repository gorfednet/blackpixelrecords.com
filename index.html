Est. 2008

Out of the Signal,
Into the Dark

Black Pixel Records has been pressing sound into the edges of Toronto's underground since the aughts. We operate where the dance floor meets the void and focus on releasing music that demands attention, holds up after midnight, and refuses to settle into the obvious.

Artists

EMH

EMH is a band that hits hard techno with the attitude of metal and the disregard of punk. Confrontational, physical, and loud, music that drags the dancefloor into the pit and dares it to keep up.

Ghettocyb.org

The original cyborg DJ. For over 25 years, Ghettocyb.org has built wearable computers and written the software to run them. Where most DJs carry a laptop, Ghettocyb.org wears the machine.

Gorf

Crafting left-field electronic music since 2003. His sound moves through downtempo breakbeats, chill electronica, with IDM, ambient, trip hop, drum and bass, jungle, and big beat folded into a style that is unmistakably his own.

Visit Website

Wolf Glitter

Dirty electro pop: grimy, fun, and high voltage as hell. On stage, the Toronto band is relentless, with big synth hooks, blown-out textures, and pure crowd-moving energy from first beat to last.
